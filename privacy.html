Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This policy explains how Etrack Video Pipeline ("the app"), operated by Etrack, Morocco, handles data when a channel owner connects a YouTube channel with their Google account. The app is used only by Etrack, on channels Etrack owns.

Data we access

When you connect a channel, you grant the app the following Google permissions:

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos to your channel.
  • youtube.force-ssl: to set captions and thumbnails, to edit the title or description of your own videos, and to read the comments on your own videos and reply to them from your channel.
  • youtube.readonly: to read the list of your videos and their public details.
  • yt-analytics.readonly and yt-analytics-monetary.readonly: to read your channel's analytics reports (views, watch time, retention, traffic sources, aggregated audience age, gender and country, and estimated revenue).

We do not access your email, contacts, Google Drive, or any data unrelated to your YouTube channel. Analytics reports are aggregated by YouTube: they never identify individual viewers.

How we use the data

  • To publish and schedule videos on your channel.
  • To measure how videos perform and improve future videos (topics, titles, thumbnails, length, publication time).
  • To fix the title or thumbnail of a video that performs poorly.
  • To reply, from the channel, to viewers whose comment asks for the free guide offered in the video. We keep only the comment ID, the video ID and the date of our reply, so that we never reply twice. We do not store commenters' names or comment text.

We do not sell the data, do not use it for advertising, and do not use it to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Storage and security

  • The OAuth access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in our private hosting environment and are never published.
  • Statistics are stored as aggregated numbers in a private repository that only Etrack can access.
  • Data is sent only over encrypted connections (HTTPS) to Google's APIs.

Sharing

We do not share Google user data with third parties. Data is transferred only to the extent needed to provide the app (for example, to Google's own APIs and to our private hosting provider), or if required by law.

Retention and deletion

  • Analytics data is kept for as long as the channel is connected, and deleted within 30 days after it is disconnected.
  • Stored YouTube API data is refreshed or deleted at least every 30 days, as required by the YouTube API Services policies.
  • You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google account's security settings. We then delete the stored tokens.
  • To ask for deletion of any data, write to the contact address below; we answer within 30 days.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy describes how Google handles your data.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

We will update the date at the top of this page when this policy changes.
