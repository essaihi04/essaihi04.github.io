Cheat sheet: 3 free AI tools to study smarter

⬇ Download the PDF

Notes in, practice exam out. Here is what each tool does, how to start in two minutes, and what to watch for.

1. NotebookLM (Google)

  • What it does: you add your own sources (notes, PDFs) and it generates flashcards and quizzes grounded in that material.
  • Added: September 2025. Available on the free plan (free-plan daily limits may apply).
  • Start: open NotebookLM, create a notebook, upload your PDF or notes, then open the Flashcards and Quiz tools.
  • Tip: use the explain button on a wrong answer to see why it was wrong.

2. Anki

  • What it does: flashcard app that schedules each card using spaced repetition.
  • Cost: the desktop app is free and open source. Syncing through AnkiWeb is free. The official iOS app is a paid one-time purchase.
  • FSRS: since Anki 23.10 you can switch the scheduler to FSRS (Settings > Scheduling > Enable FSRS). Anki's FAQ says it needs fewer reviews than the default algorithm for the same retention.
  • Start: install Anki on desktop, make a deck from your NotebookLM flashcards, enable FSRS, review a few minutes a day.

3. Gemini Guided Learning

  • What it does: a Gemini mode that acts as a tutor. It breaks problems into steps and explains the reasoning instead of just giving the answer.
  • Start: in the Gemini app, pick Guided Learning next to the prompt box, then paste the topic or problem you are stuck on.

A simple evening routine

  1. Upload your notes to NotebookLM and generate a quiz.
  2. Move the cards you miss into Anki.
  3. Ask Gemini Guided Learning to walk you through the topics you keep missing.
