How AI Works: The 1-Page Cheat Sheet

⬇ Download the PDF

A large language model (LLM) like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Llama does one thing: it guesses the next piece of text, over and over.

The core idea in 4 steps

  1. Your text is cut into tokens. A token is a chunk of text, about 3/4 of an English word (OpenAI).
  2. The model reads every token so far: your whole conversation, not just the last few words.
  3. It scores every possible next token and picks one.
  4. It adds that token and starts again. One token at a time, that is the whole answer.

Phone keyboard vs. ChatGPT

| | Phone keyboard | Large language model |

|---|---|---|

| Job | Guess the next word | Guess the next token |

| Model size | Tiny, runs on your phone | Huge, usually runs in a data center |

| What it reads | What you are typing | Your entire conversation |

| Training text | Small | Trillions of tokens |

The scale, in numbers

  • Meta's Llama 3 was pretrained on over 15 trillion tokens of public text (Meta AI, April 2024).
  • 15 trillion tokens ≈ 11 trillion words. At the average adult reading speed of 238 words per minute (Brysbaert, 2019), a person reading nonstop would need about 90,000 years.

What nobody taught it

During pretraining, the model is never given grammar rules, a fact list or coding lessons. It only practices one game: predict the next token. Grammar, facts and code patterns are what it picked up along the way. (Chat assistants then get extra training on example conversations.)

3 things this explains

  • Why answers vary: each word is a pick among likely options, so the same question can get different answers.
  • Why AI can sound sure and still be wrong: a likely-sounding next word is not a checked fact. Verify anything important.
  • Why long chats get forgetful: the model can only read a limited number of tokens at once (its context window).

Try it yourself (free)

  • Ask any chatbot: *"Finish this sentence 3 different ways: I'll be there in…"* and compare.
  • Paste your text into OpenAI's free tokenizer page to see how it is cut into tokens.
