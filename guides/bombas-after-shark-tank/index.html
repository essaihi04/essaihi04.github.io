The deal

| | |

|---|---|

| Founders | David Heath and Randy Goldberg |

| Shark Tank | Season 6, 2014 |

| The ask | $200,000 for 5% (a $4 million valuation) |

| The deal | $200,000 for 17.5% from Daymond John (about a $1.14 million valuation) |

| Sharks who passed | Kevin O'Leary, Robert Herjavec, Lori Greiner, Mark Cuban |

Before and after

  • Feb 2011: Heath reads that socks are the most requested clothing item in homeless shelters.
  • Aug 2013: Indiegogo campaign; about $145,000 raised.
  • 2014: about $1 million from friends, family and angels.
  • 2018: revenue passes $100 million.
  • 2023: lifetime sales over $1 billion; over 100 million items donated to 3,500+ organizations.
  • Oct 2025: first physical store opens in New York City.

3 things the company did

  1. Proved demand (crowdfunding) before pitching.
  2. Kept one clear mission: one for one.
  3. Stayed focused on socks, as its investor advised.

2 mistakes it avoided

  1. Chasing many products at once.
  2. Letting the mission replace product quality.

Glossary

  • Equity: a slice of ownership of a company.
  • Valuation: the price of the whole company (money invested ÷ share received).
  • Margin: the share of each sale you keep after the cost of the product.
  • Due diligence: the investor's line-by-line check of the books and claims before a deal closes.
