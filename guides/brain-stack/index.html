Your Free Brain Stack: Step-by-Step Guide

⬇ Download the PDF

Replace one hour of scrolling with one hour of learning, using free AI tools and one document you already own.

Before you start

  • Pick ONE source: a lecture PDF, a work report or a long article (a public page saved as PDF works too).
  • Put your phone in another room for the hour.
  • Tool names change. Google renamed NotebookLM to Gemini Notebook in July 2026, so you may see either name. Free usage limits also change: check the usage page in the app.

The hour (60 minutes)

| Minutes | Step | Tool |

|---|---|---|

| 5 | Read the study guide | NotebookLM / Gemini Notebook, Studio, Report |

| 15 | Take the quiz without looking | Studio, Quiz |

| 10 | Read the mind map, find the empty branches | Studio, Mind map |

| 10 | Ask a chatbot for a 20-minute plan | Gemini or ChatGPT (free) |

| 20 | Retry the questions you missed | Same quiz |

Step by step

  1. Upload one source. NotebookLM accepts PDFs, websites, Google Docs and YouTube transcripts. Answers come from your files, with pointers to the page they came from.
  2. Study guide. In Studio, make a Report and choose Study guide (the help page also lists Briefing document and FAQ).
  3. Quiz. In Studio, generate a Quiz. Answer in your head first, then check. A wrong answer is the thing to learn.
  4. Mind map. Generate it, then note which branches you keep missing.
  5. Plan. Paste the weak spots into a free chatbot with the prompt below.
  6. Flashcards. Only for questions you got wrong twice.

Prompts to copy

  • I have 20 minutes a day. Here are the 3 topics I missed in my quiz: [paste]. Give me a plan for tomorrow with one small task per topic.
  • Explain [topic] to a 10-year-old using only the text of my document, then ask me one question about it.
  • Turn the topics I got wrong into 5 short flashcards (question on one side, answer on the other).
  • Here is my plan: [paste]. Cut it down to what fits in 20 minutes.

The 30-day plan

  • Week 1: one source, one full run. Learn the loop.
  • Week 2: add flashcards for terms you keep forgetting.
  • Week 3: switch to a new source so the habit does not depend on one topic.
  • Week 4: retake the quiz on your first document and compare your score.

What can go wrong

  • Free limits change and sources online disagree. Check the usage page in the app and come back later if you hit a limit.
  • A quiz can contain a bad question. If an answer looks wrong, check the page it points to: your document wins.
  • A chatbot plan is a draft. You decide; cut anything too big.

Tip

Say each answer out loud in your own words. If you cannot say it simply, you have not learned it yet.
