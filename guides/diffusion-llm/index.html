Diffusion LLMs: the one-page cheat sheet

⬇ Download the PDF

Why some new AI models write the whole answer at once, what they are good at, and what is still unproven.

Two ways to write a sentence

  • Autoregressive (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini): the model writes one token (a word piece) at a time, left to right. "A token cannot be generated until all the text that comes before it has been generated." (Inception Labs)
  • Diffusion: the model starts from random placeholder tokens, then refines the whole block in a few passes until the text settles. It can "error correct during the generation process." (Google DeepMind)
  • Same family of technique as the diffusion models behind AI images and video (Sora, Midjourney). (Inception Labs)

The numbers (with sources)

  • DiffusionGemma (Google, June 10, 2026): open model, Apache 2.0, 26B mixture-of-experts with about 3.8B active parameters. Drafts 256 tokens per forward pass. Up to 4x faster, 1,000+ tokens per second on one NVIDIA H100, 700+ on an RTX 5090. (Google blog, model page)
  • Gemini Diffusion (Google DeepMind, experimental demo): 1,479 tokens per second average sampling speed, excluding 0.84 s overhead. (DeepMind)
  • Mercury 2 (Inception Labs, Feb 24, 2026): reasoning model, about 1,009 tokens per second on NVIDIA Blackwell GPUs, according to the company. (Inception, The Decoder)

What is still unproven

  1. Quality: "DiffusionGemma's overall output quality is lower than standard Gemma 4." (Google)
  2. Scale: Google says the speed advantage is strongest at low-to-medium batch sizes on a single accelerator; big cloud serving gains less.
  3. Macs: unified-memory hardware such as Apple Silicon "may not see the same acceleration."
  4. Many speed figures come from the companies themselves; independent benchmarks are still few.

Where diffusion text models make sense today

  • Fast autocomplete and code edits, where latency matters more than the last bit of quality.
  • Voice assistants that must answer in under 2 seconds.
  • Drafts you will review anyway.

Try it yourself (free)

  • DiffusionGemma weights are on Hugging Face and Kaggle (needs a GPU with about 24 GB of memory when quantized, per Google).
  • Compare: ask the same question to a normal chatbot and a diffusion model, and time both.

Glossary

  • Token: a piece of a word; about 3/4 of an English word on average.
  • Forward pass: one run of the model over its input.
  • Tokens per second: how fast the model writes. 1,000 tokens/s is roughly 750 words every second.
