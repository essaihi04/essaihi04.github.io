The AI Fact-Check Cheat Sheet

⬇ Download the PDF

AI chatbots sometimes state false things with total confidence. This is called a hallucination. Here is why it happens and five quick checks to catch it.

Why it happens (30-second version)

  • A chatbot does not look facts up. It predicts the next word that sounds right. A fake fact can sound exactly like a real one.
  • OpenAI researchers (Kalai et al., September 2025) found that "language models hallucinate because the training and evaluation procedures reward guessing over acknowledging uncertainty". A lucky guess scores a point; "I don't know" scores zero.

The real case from the video

  • In *Mata v. Avianca* (New York federal court, 2023), a lawyer filed a brief citing six court decisions that did not exist. ChatGPT had invented them. When asked if they were real, it said yes.
  • On June 22, 2023, Judge P. Kevin Castel fined the two lawyers and their firm $5,000 in total.

5 checks before you trust an answer

  1. The 3-chat test. Ask the same question in three new chats. Facts the model really knows stay the same; invented details (names, dates, numbers, quotes) tend to change. This is the idea behind SelfCheckGPT (Manakul et al., 2023).
  2. Open every source. Ask for links, then click them. A real title on a page that does not exist is a classic hallucination.
  3. Check the specifics first. Exact numbers, dates, quotes, citations and names are where errors hide. Verify those before the general idea.
  4. Give it permission to say "I don't know". Add to your prompt: *"If you are not sure, say so. Do not guess."*
  5. Use search-backed answers for facts. For anything recent or important, use a tool that shows its sources, and read them.

Copy-paste prompts

  • Answer only if you are confident. If not, reply "I'm not sure" and explain what I should check.
  • List every factual claim in your answer, with a source link for each one. Mark any claim you cannot source.
  • Re-read your previous answer and point out anything that might be wrong or made up.
