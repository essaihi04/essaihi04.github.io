Coffee Meets Bagel: the Shark Tank deal sheet

⬇ Download the PDF

The deal at a glance

  • Show: Shark Tank US, Season 6 (aired January 9, 2015)
  • The ask: $500,000 for 5% of the company, which values it at $10 million ($500,000 ÷ 5%)
  • The offer: Mark Cuban offered $30 million to buy the entire company, reported as the largest offer in the show's history at the time. Some outlets note it may have been more hypothetical than a firm offer.
  • The answer: the founders, the Kang sisters, declined right away
  • After the show: a $7.8 million Series A in 2015, led by DCM Ventures, with Azure Capital and Quest Ventures
  • Today: the app is still operating on iOS and Android

3 things this case shows

  1. Know your upside before you sell. The founders said the market offered a far bigger opportunity than a $30 million exit.
  2. A refusal can be followed by outside funding. Within weeks of the episode, venture investors put in $7.8 million.
  3. Valuation is just price divided by share. $500,000 for 5% means $10 million. A buyout of 100% for $30 million is a different kind of deal.

2 mistakes to avoid

  1. Reading one headline number as the whole story. Reports disagree on how firm the $30 million offer really was.
  2. Assuming a refusal always works out. This case is one outcome, not a rule. The company is still running, but this guide makes no claim about revenue or valuation.

Glossary

  • Valuation: what a company is worth, implied by the price paid for a share of it.
  • Equity: the percentage of the company an investor owns.
  • Series A: a first major round of venture funding.
  • Buyout / acquisition: buying 100% of a company.
