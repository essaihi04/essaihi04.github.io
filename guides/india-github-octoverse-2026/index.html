India vs the US on GitHub: the Octoverse 2026 numbers + 4 open source tools from India

👉 Open the link⬇ Download the PDF

Everything in this guide comes from GitHub's Octoverse reports and the repositories themselves. Star counts checked on November 14, 2026.

The numbers (Octoverse 2026, year to September 30, 2026)

  • Developers on GitHub worldwide: 225 million+
  • United States: 38.3M (+10.30M in a year, +37%)
  • India: 32.0M (+10.08M in a year, +46%)
  • Gap between them: 6.1M → 6.3M (it grew, even though India grew faster)
  • Next: China 12.4M, Brazil 10.2M (+3.28M), Japan 7.15M, UK 6.47M, Indonesia 6.34M, Germany 6.30M
  • GitHub's 2030 projection: India 57.5M, US 54.7M. Distance covered after one year: US 38.6%, India 28.3%
  • GitHub ran 43 forecasting models to 2031 and does not give a year for India passing the US
  • Locations are self-reported by developers: these are accounts, not a census

How the prediction changed

  1. Octoverse 2024: India "on track to surpass the United States... by 2028" (earlier estimate: 2027)
  2. Octoverse 2025: "poised to overtake" the US "within the next few years", no year
  3. Octoverse 2026: 43 models, no date

4 open source tools that started in India

  1. Hoppscotch: open source API client, a free alternative to Postman. Runs in the browser, can be self-hosted. MIT license, ~80.8K stars. github.com/hoppscotch/hoppscotch
  2. ERPNext: free and open source ERP (accounting, inventory, sales, payroll, manufacturing) by Frappe Technologies. GPL-3.0, ~41.3K stars. github.com/frappe/erpnext
  3. Appsmith: build admin panels, internal tools and dashboards on top of 25+ databases and APIs. Apache-2.0, ~41.3K stars. github.com/appsmithorg/appsmith
  4. listmonk: self-hosted newsletter and mailing list manager, a single binary with PostgreSQL. AGPL-3.0, ~24.3K stars. github.com/knadh/listmonk

Try one in 2 minutes

  • Hoppscotch: open hoppscotch.io, paste https://api.github.com/repos/hoppscotch/hoppscotch, press Send, and read stargazers_count in the response.
  • For the self-hosted tools, follow each repository's own install docs (Docker setups are documented in each README).

The agent year (Octoverse 2026)

  • More than 40% of pull requests in September 2026 were started by an AI agent (17% in March): 110M in one month
  • Commits: 34.3B in a year (12.9B the year before)
  • TypeScript is the most used language (+3.62M monthly contributors), then Python (+3.23M); Rust grew fastest (+162%)
