Why Microsoft Is Restarting Three Mile Island: the map and every source

⬇ Download the PDF

A one-page guide to the AI Explained Simply Short. Every number below has its source.

The plant

  • Three Mile Island Unit 1, Londonderry Township, Dauphin County, Pennsylvania, on the Susquehanna River, about 12 miles from Harrisburg.
  • The 1979 partial meltdown happened in Unit 2, not Unit 1. Unit 2 has been shut since 1979 and is being decommissioned by Energy Solutions.
  • Unit 1 shut down on 20 September 2019 for economic reasons. In its final year it ran at full power 96.3% of the time.
  • Renamed the Crane Clean Energy Center, after former Exelon CEO Chris Crane.

The deal

  • Announced 20 September 2024: a 20-year power purchase agreement. Microsoft buys the plant's output to match the power its data centers in the PJM grid use with carbon-free energy.
  • About 835 MW, enough for more than 800,000 average homes (Constellation).
  • Restart cost: about $1.6 billion (Constellation). The U.S. Department of Energy announced a $1 billion loan for the restart in 2025.
  • Target: first announced for 2028, moved to as early as 2027 after the grid operator PJM approved an early interconnection request. The NRC's final decision on the restart is expected in 2027.

Why here

  1. Same grid as Data Center Alley. Three Mile Island and Loudoun County, Virginia (the biggest data center cluster on Earth) are both in PJM, which runs the grid in all or parts of 13 states and Washington, D.C. The great-circle distance between the two is about 140 km.
  2. The plant already exists. Georgia's two newest reactors, Vogtle 3 and 4, took about 15 years (construction began in 2009, Unit 4 entered service on 29 April 2024) and cost more than $30 billion.
  3. The scale of AI power. The IEA says a hyperscale AI-focused data center can have a capacity of 100 MW or more, using as much electricity a year as 100,000 households. 835 MW / 100 MW ≈ 8 such data centers.

Glossary

  • MW (megawatt): one million watts, a measure of power at one instant.
  • PPA (power purchase agreement): a long contract to buy electricity from a given plant at an agreed price.
  • PJM: the regional grid operator for the mid-Atlantic and part of the Midwest.
