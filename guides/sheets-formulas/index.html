5 Prompts to Get Spreadsheet Formulas From Gemini in Google Sheets

Describe what you want in plain English, then check the result. Free to copy.

How to open Gemini in Sheets (Google Docs Editors Help)

  1. Open a spreadsheet in Google Sheets on your computer.
  2. Click Ask Gemini at the top right (or type = in a cell, then press Ctrl + Alt + G on Windows/ChromeOS, or Cmd + Ctrl + G on Mac).
  3. Describe the formula you need. You can use cell references or generic names.
  4. Click the target cell and choose Insert. Choose Retry for a different version.
  5. If a cell shows an error: hover over it and click Fix.

Availability depends on your Google Workspace or Google AI plan.

5 prompts to copy

  1. Create a formula that divides goals by games. (Google's own help-page example)
  2. Sum the values in column C where column B is "West". (usually a SUMIF)
  3. Count how many cells in column A are not empty.
  4. Show the highest value in column D for each region in column B.
  5. Explain this formula in simple words: <paste your formula>

Tip: name your columns in the prompt ("sales in column C") so Gemini can match your sheet.

Always check the result

Google says Gemini may suggest inaccurate information, so review suggestions before relying on them. Test the formula by hand on two rows before you copy it down a whole column. Do not use it as a substitute for professional financial or legal advice.
