The AI Chip Map: Why Almost Every AI Chip Is Made in Taiwan

⬇ Download the PDF

A one-page map of where an advanced AI chip comes from, with every number sourced. Companion to the AI Explained Simply video.

The chain in 4 steps

  1. Design (USA): Nvidia designs Blackwell in Santa Clara, California. Nvidia owns no factory that makes it ("fabless").
  2. Machine (the Netherlands): ASML, in Veldhoven, is the only company that sells EUV lithography machines. A laser hits drops of molten tin up to 50,000 times per second to make 13.5 nm light. Reuters puts one machine at about $250 million.
  3. Make (Taiwan): TSMC prints the chip on silicon wafers. Blackwell uses TSMC's 4NP process. Advanced fabs sit in Hsinchu (north) and Tainan (south, Fab 18: 5 nm and 3 nm).
  4. Package (Taiwan, for now): each chip is joined to its memory with TSMC's CoWoS advanced packaging. In 2025, wafers made in Arizona still flew back to Taiwan for this step.

Key numbers

  • 69.9%: TSMC's share of global foundry revenue in 2025 (TrendForce), $122.5 billion in sales. Samsung was second with 7.2%.
  • 58%: share of TSMC's 2025 revenue from high-performance computing, mostly AI.
  • 92%: Taiwan's share of the world's most advanced (below 10 nm) chipmaking capacity in 2021 (BCG x SIA).
  • 58,000 m²: clean room in each phase of Fab 18, about double a standard logic fab. Total investment: more than NT$1.86 trillion.
  • $165 billion: TSMC's total planned investment in Arizona (March 2025): 6 fabs, 2 advanced packaging plants, 1 R&D centre.

Timeline

  • Dec 15, 1980: Hsinchu Science Park opens.
  • 1985: Morris Chang (25 years at Texas Instruments) is asked to lead Taiwan's ITRI.
  • Feb 21, 1987: TSMC is founded in Hsinchu, the first "pure-play" foundry: it makes chips for others and never competes with its customers.
  • 1993: Nvidia is founded, without a factory.
  • Dec 29, 2022: 3 nm volume production starts at Fab 18, Tainan.
  • Feb 24, 2024: TSMC opens its first Japanese fab in Kumamoto (partners: Sony, Denso, Toyota), for older chips, not AI.
  • Apr 3, 2024: strongest quake in Taiwan in about 25 years. More than 70% of TSMC's tools were running again within 10 hours.
  • Jul 2025: Rapidus (Japan) shows its first 2 nm test chips in Chitose, Hokkaido. Mass production goal: 2027.
  • Oct 2025: first US-made Nvidia Blackwell wafer at TSMC Arizona.

3 things to remember

  1. A business model: make chips for everyone, compete with no one. So every chip designer came.
  2. A cluster: engineers, suppliers and repair teams within an hour's drive. One factory abroad cannot copy that.
  3. Scale: 70% of the market means more practice and fewer mistakes than anyone else.

Glossary

  • Foundry: a factory that makes chips designed by other companies.
  • Fabless: a chip company that designs but does not manufacture (Nvidia, AMD, Apple).
  • EUV lithography: printing a chip's pattern with extreme ultraviolet light (13.5 nm).
  • Advanced packaging (CoWoS): joining the chip and its memory so they work as one AI chip.
  • nm (nanometre): a billionth of a metre; the name of a process generation (5 nm, 3 nm, 2 nm).
