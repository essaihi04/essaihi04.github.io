Turn Any PDF Into a Quiz With AI: the prompt pack

⬇ Download the PDF

Works with any free AI chat that accepts file uploads. No paid tool needed.

The 3 steps

  1. Open a free AI chat.
  2. Upload your PDF (notes, a chapter, a paper).
  3. Paste the main prompt below.

Main prompt (copy and paste)

Write 10 multiple choice questions from this document.
Four options each (A, B, C, D).
Mark the right answer and explain why in one sentence.
For every answer, quote the exact page or passage it comes from.

Follow-up prompts

  • Retry what you missed: Quiz me again, only on the questions I got wrong. Use different wording.
  • Harder version: Now write 10 harder questions that need me to combine two ideas from the document.
  • Flashcards: Turn the key terms in this document into flashcards: term on one side, simple definition on the other.
  • Exam mode: Ask me one question at a time, wait for my answer, then tell me if I was right.

Why the "quote the page" line matters

AI chats can invent answers. Asking for the exact page lets you check each answer against your own document in seconds. If the quote is not in your PDF, discard that question.

2 mistakes to avoid

  • Trusting an answer with no page quote.
  • Uploading a scanned PDF with no readable text: the AI cannot read it, so run it through OCR first.

Privacy

Do not upload documents with private or confidential information to a free chat.
