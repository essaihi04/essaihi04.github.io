Summarize a 1-Hour Meeting in 1 Minute With Free AI: the Prompt Pack

⬇ Download the PDF

A short, copy-and-paste method. No numbers to trust, just a workflow you can check yourself.

The 3 steps

  1. Record the call with the free recorder built into your meeting app (ask everyone's permission first), and save the audio.
  2. Transcribe: drop the audio file into a free speech-to-text tool. You get the full transcript.
  3. Summarize: paste the transcript into any free AI chat with one of the prompts below.

Prompt 1: the basic summary

Summarize this meeting in five bullet points. List every decision, and who owns each task.

Prompt 2: the verification line (the trick)

For every point, quote the exact line from the transcript that supports it.

Now you can check each point. If a quote is not in the transcript, the AI made it up: delete that point.

Prompt 3: action items only

From this transcript, list every action item as: task, owner, deadline (write "not stated" if no deadline was said).

Prompt 4: follow-up email

Write a short follow-up email from this summary: decisions first, then action items with owners.

Prompt 5: what was left open

List every question or topic that was raised but not decided. Quote the line for each.

Good habits

  • Always get everyone's permission before you record.
  • AI summaries can be wrong: always read the quoted lines for important decisions.
  • Do not paste confidential meetings into a tool your company has not approved.
