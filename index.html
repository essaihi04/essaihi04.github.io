Educational videos, produced and published with care

Etrack Video Pipeline is an internal tool run by Etrack (Morocco). It uploads, schedules and measures videos on the YouTube channels that Etrack itself owns and operates.

What the app does

  • Uploads finished videos, with their title, description, captions and thumbnail, to our own channels, and schedules their publication.
  • Reads the public data and the YouTube Analytics reports of those same channels (views, watch time, click-through rate, audience retention) so that we can improve the next videos.
  • Updates the title or thumbnail of our own videos when the statistics show they can be clearer.
  • Reads the public comments posted on our own videos and replies, from our channel, to viewers who ask for the free guide mentioned in the video (for example by commenting a keyword such as "REPO").

The app is not offered to the public. Only the owner of each channel signs in, with their own Google account, to connect that channel.

Our channels

  • Fallen Empires: the rise and fall of companies and empires.
  • AI Explained Simply: artificial intelligence explained in plain language.
  • Lost Histories: forgotten stories from history.
  • Cosmos Daily: space and astronomy.
  • Ranked: ranked comparisons and countdowns.
  • Repo Radar: trending open-source projects on GitHub.

Every video lists its sources in the description. Scenes that reconstruct real events are labelled as altered or synthetic content on YouTube.

Use of Google and YouTube data

The app uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API. By using it, channel owners agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy, and ours in our Privacy Policy.
